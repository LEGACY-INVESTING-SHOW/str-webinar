Where the tax bill sits

Most of your investing starts after the IRS has already been paid.

You earn, tax comes out of the paycheck, and whatever is left gets invested. Your 401(k) only ever sees the money that survived the tax bill.

A short-term rental you materially participate in can sit inside the tax strategy instead of after it. If you pay $40,000 or $50,000 a year in federal income tax, that changes the math on where your next $50,000 investment goes.
